Private integration

Executor Personal Integrations

A private integration operated by Christian Alares at Hidden Village to connect his Google accounts to Executor running on his own computer.

Executor makes account tools available to clients the owner connects, including AI assistants. This page explains the integration. It does not provide a public sign-up or access to any mailbox.

What the integration can do

  • Identify the Google account being connected using basic profile and email information.
  • Find and read Gmail messages and attachments.
  • Create drafts, send messages, manage labels, archive messages, and move messages to Trash.

The Gmail permission is gmail.modify. It does not grant the separate permission to permanently delete messages while bypassing Trash or to change Gmail account settings.

How access works

Each account owner signs in directly with Google and approves access. Executor stores the resulting connection locally. The private integration does not ask for a Google password. Information returned by account tools may be processed by the connected client or AI provider as part of the owner's requested task.

Read the privacy policy for details about data access, storage, sharing, and removing access.
