Privacy policy

Updated 18 September 2026

This policy covers Executor Personal Integrations, a private Google integration operated by Christian Alares at Hidden Village. It is intended for the operator's own accounts and is not a public email service.

Data accessed and its purpose

With the account owner's authorization, the integration accesses basic Google profile information and the account's email address to identify the connection. It can access Gmail messages, headers, recipients, attachments, threads, drafts, and labels to carry out requested email tasks.

The integration uses this information to search, read, summarize, draft, send, label, archive, and move email to Trash as directed by the account owner. It does not request permission to permanently delete messages while bypassing Trash or to change Gmail account settings.

Storage and retention

Executor runs on the operator's computer and stores the Google connection credentials there so it can maintain authorized access. Mailbox data remains in Google unless a requested action changes it or copies information elsewhere. Tool results, downloaded attachments, logs, and conversation history may create additional copies on the computer or in a connected client.

Connection credentials are retained while the connection is in use. Copies of tool results and conversation history follow the retention settings of the application where they are stored. Removing a connection does not automatically delete previous conversations, downloaded files, or other copies.

Connected clients and AI services

Executor can return Google account data to clients that the operator connects, including AI assistants. When such a client uses a hosted AI service, relevant message content or attachments may be sent to that provider to perform the requested task. Running Executor locally does not mean that all information remains on the computer.

Google user data may only be transferred as needed to provide the requested user-facing functionality and in accordance with Google's Limited Use requirements. The operator must use provider settings and terms that do not permit Google user data to be used to train generalized AI or machine-learning models. Data is not sold or used for advertising.

Security and control

Google sign-in takes place on Google's authorization pages. The integration receives OAuth credentials, not the account's Google password. Access to the computer, Executor, and its connected clients must be limited to authorized users.

You can revoke access through Google Account connections and remove the connection from Executor. To remove saved copies, delete them from the relevant local files and connected applications. For help with data access or deletion, contact christian@hiddenvillage.se.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Executor Personal Integrations' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

This information website

These information pages do not connect to Google accounts or collect mailbox content. The website hosting provider processes normal web requests, which include information such as IP address and browser request metadata.
