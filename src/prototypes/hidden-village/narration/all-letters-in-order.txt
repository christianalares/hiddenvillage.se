1. INVITATION

For the one who wandered in.

Hi, I’m Christian. You found my little corner of the internet.

Some of me is scattered here. A thing that did not exist. A question without an answer. A rhythm that followed me home.

Three marks. Three places. Gather them if you like. The last light has a letter for you.

Begin where the wind has nothing left to lean against.


2. CURIOSITY

What passes between things?

A lot of my curiosity goes into AI. I keep joining it to the tools around me, wondering what might happen between them.

Sometimes a useful answer. Sometimes a better question. Both are reasons to leave the notebook open.

Look through the brass eye. Then seek the room that remembers a rhythm.


3. MAKING

It began with a missing thing.

A task with too many steps. An idea with nowhere to stay. Two things that ought to speak.

That is usually how I begin building software. I make the thing I wish I had, then keep returning to the parts that do not feel quite right.

The small details leave the longest shadows.

Keep this mark. Another waits above the trees.


4. RHYTHM

The room remembers.

Before these keys, there were others. I used to produce music. I still play drums. Some habits keep their own time.

Beyond this room, the blue court is slowly returning to the woods. I love a game of padel. Mine are usually on less overgrown courts.

Three marks belong together. Carry them to the last light.


5. HOME

A light left on.

Home is Stockholm, with my family. This is another sort of home. My little corner of the internet.

The paths bend. The work is rarely finished. There is usually something I meant to come back to.

If you found a little of me along the way, the place is doing its job. Make yourself at home.

Three marks open the folded letter beneath this one.


6. LETTER

You took the long way.

A missing thing, a question, a rhythm. A few parts of the same person.

Thank you for spending a little time here. I hope you found something that made you curious.

You know where to find me. Leave a word if you like.
Christian
