# Padel court reference

Checked 2026-10-15. Authority: [FIP Rules of Padel, effective 2026-01-01, printed pages 4–8](https://www.padelfip.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/FIP_Rules-of-Padel-1.pdf).

## Current dimensions

| Element | FIP specification |
| --- | --- |
| Playing rectangle | 10 × 20 m internally; 0.5% tolerance. |
| Ends | 3 m rebound wall/glass, then 1 m mesh; 4 m total. |
| Variant 2 Crystal sides | At each end: 4 m-long, 3 m-high glass return. Mesh extends to 4 m over the outermost 2 m. |
| Service lines | 6.95 m from net, parallel to it. |
| Centre service line | Bisects service boxes; extends at least 0.20 m beyond service lines. |
| Markings | 0.05 m wide, symmetrical. |
| Net | 10 m long; 0.88 m centre, 0.92 m ends; posts ≤1.05 m. White tape 0.05–0.063 m. |
| Fence | Welded square mesh, diagonal openings 0.05–0.0708 m; recommended wire 0.002–0.003 m. |
| Access | Long sides, symmetric about centre. One opening/side: 1.05–2.20 m wide. Two/side: each 0.72–1.10 m. Height 2.00–2.20 m. Doors optional. |
| Surface | Blue permitted. |
| Overhead clearance | ≥6 m, 8 m suggested for new courts. |

All dimensions above refer to the [2026 FIP rules](https://www.padelfip.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/FIP_Rules-of-Padel-1.pdf), not a model seller's illustration.

## Diagram cross-check

The 2026 PDF references diagrams but does not display them. The [older official illustrated rules, revision 2021-05-28](https://www.padelfip.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/2-game-regulations.pdf), printed pages 4, 7 and 9, were inspected visually. The old **Variant 1 Crystal** drawing matches the description now called **Variant 2 Crystal**: middle 12 m is 3 m mesh; glass returns remain full height. The access drawings place openings alongside the net, not in a back corner. Use current dimensions above, because older access maxima differ.

## Recommended scene implementation

These are modeling choices, not additional regulations:

- Use the modern glass arrangement above, with slender dark posts, subtle glass reflections and weathering concentrated around the bottom edges. Keep the blue surface visible through the enclosure.
- Local origin is the net centre, width along X and length along Z. Inside limits: X ±5, Z ±10. Glass returns occupy Z −10…−6 and +6…+10; upper side mesh only Z −10…−8 and +8…+10. Central side mesh occupies Z −6…+6.
- Put two open entrances on each long side, one either side of the net post. Use approximately 1.00 m clear width after padding and 2.20 m clear height each. Use a rounded central post and readable open gaps, without a low bar across the walking route.
- Make the approach visible from the woodland path: a broad worn track, supported ramp or gently graded earth approach, and a landing level with the court. The camera should follow this entrance, not pass through fencing or climb the retaining wall. Check the whole approach against the actual terrain height.
- Keep encroaching plants, fallen twigs and leaf litter around the margins. Preserve a clear entrance and recognizable service boxes. Do not place full trees through the playing surface.
- Set service stripes at Z ±6.95. Centre stripe at X=0 ends at Z ±7.15. The net sits at Z=0, with a slight sag between the end posts. Avoid adding a tennis-style perimeter stripe.
- Decorative abandoned vegetation makes this a narrative environment, not a claim that the fictional court is maintained for sanctioned play.
